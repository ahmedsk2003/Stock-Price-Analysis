# 📊 Stock Market Analysis Dashboards (NSE India)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive visual analysis of stock market performance on the **National Stock Exchange (NSE)** using interactive dashboard design. It features two core perspectives:

1. **Single Stock Deep Dive:** An in-depth performance analysis of **Adani Enterprises** over a 1-year period.


2. **Multi-Stock Exchange Overview:** A macro-level market breadth and volatility analysis across **50 major NSE stocks**.



---

## 📈 Dashboard 1: Adani Enterprises Stock Performance (1 Year)

A dark-themed analysis focused on the price action, daily returns, trading volume, and short-term trends for **Adani Enterprises** built from official NSE market data.

### **Key Metrics & Features:**

* **Executive KPI Cards:**
* **Current Price:** ₹290.73K


* **52-Week High Close:** ₹3.21K


* **52-Week Low Close:** ₹1.85K


* **Total Volume:** 183.43M




* **Technical Trend Analysis:**
* **Moving Average:** Dual-line tracking comparing short-term vs. long-term price dynamics.


* **Candlestick Chart:** Granular daily open-high-low-close view identifying bullish and bearish sessions.


* **Stock Price Trend:** Line trajectory filterable by custom timeframe (e.g., last 60 weeks).




* **Statistical Insights:**
* **Daily Return Distribution:** Histogram showing daily percentage return frequencies centered tightly between **-3% and +3%**.


* **Volume Analysis:** Daily volume bar breakdown highlighting historical liquidity surges peaked above **8.00M shares**.

## 🏛️ Dashboard 2: Stock Exchange Analysis (50 NSE Stocks)

A macro dashboard evaluating market sentiment, gainers vs. losers ratio, volatility, and volume distribution across **50 major NSE stocks**.

### **Key Metrics & Features:**

* **Market Breadth KPIs:**
* **Total Analyzed Stocks:** 50


* **Gainers vs. Losers:** 4 Gainers (8%) vs. 46 Losers (92%)


* **Average Market Change:** -2.93% (indicating overall bearish sentiment)




* **Comparative Visualizations:**
* **Highest Volume Stock:** Identifies top-traded stocks led heavily by **TATAMOTORS** (>500 Lacs volume).


* **Top Gainers & Losers:** Diverging bar chart profiling top performers (**CIPLA +7.23%**) vs. largest pullbacks (**JSWSTEEL -7.48%**).


* **Volatility Analysis (52-Week Price Range):** Top 5 volatile stocks measured by 52-week price spread, led by **NESTLEIND (4,607 range)**.


* **Volume vs. % Change Scatter Plot:** Multi-dimensional matrix mapping volume against performance to spot high-conviction market outliers.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Defensive Sector Resilience:** During broader market pullbacks (where **92% of market stocks were down**), Healthcare/Pharma stocks like **CIPLA**, **DRREDDY**, and **DIVISLAB** bucked the trend to remain in positive territory.


2. **High-Volume Volatility:** High-volume leaders like **TATAMOTORS** showed high susceptibility to negative market movement, whereas consumer staples like **NESTLEIND** exhibited wider 52-week range spreads despite lower daily percentage swings.
